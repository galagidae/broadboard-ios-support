# BroadBoard Support

Thanks for using BroadBoard! It's built and maintained by one independent developer, so when you get in touch you're talking directly to the person who wrote it. I read every message and will get back to you as soon as I can.

## Getting in touch

- **Email:** [anthony.benbrook@gmail.com](mailto:anthony.benbrook@gmail.com)
- **Bug reports and feature requests:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/galagidae/broadboard-ios-support/issues)

If you're reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, the keyboard layout you're using, and what you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

**BroadBoard doesn't show up when I tap the globe key.**
The keyboard has to be added in Settings first. Open the BroadBoard app and tap **Tap to Enable**, or go to **Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard** and choose **BroadBoard**. Then, in any app, touch and hold the globe key and select **BroadBoard**.

**Why isn't BroadBoard available in some text fields?**
iOS always switches to the system keyboard for password fields and certain other secure fields. This is an iOS restriction that applies to all third-party keyboards.

**How do I reach keys that aren't on screen?**
Drag or fling the keyboard in any direction to pan around. The keys stay large so they never have to shrink to fit.

**Can I change the size or colors?**
Yes. Open the BroadBoard app to choose a keyboard size, color theme, and layout.

**Does BroadBoard need "Allow Full Access"?**
No. BroadBoard never asks for Full Access, has no network access, and collects no data. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

## Accessibility feedback

BroadBoard was made for people with low vision, and feedback from people who use it every day is the most valuable kind. If something is hard to see, hard to reach, or doesn't work well with VoiceOver, please let me know.
